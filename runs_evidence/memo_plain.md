## What we recommend
Focus on usability, and fix the ads first. Free users say ads appear too often between songs (usability.u1).

## Why
- "Priority score" means number of complaints x how serious each is on a 1-5 scale. Usability has the biggest total across all areas: 29,301 complaints with a score of 67,460.
- The ads issue has 14,770 complaints (C009) and a priority score of 31,401 (C010). Average severity is 2.13 (C011).
- Two related usability problems sit next to it: free users can't pick or replay songs (6,758 complaints, C013), and menus and playlists were removed or broken (6,260 complaints, C017).
- Example reviews: d2f3874f-d15d-4a2f-bb97-4f1213defa46 and 3e8bdc92-4934-457e-85cc-8b9d14bf51c0.

## Other options we looked at
- **General criticism (other.o1):** 31,775 complaints (C001), score 63,448 (C004). It ranks first, but the reviews just say "bad app" and name no problem, so there is nothing specific to fix.
- **Features locked behind premium (billing.b3):** 12,743 complaints (C005), score 34,611 (C006), average severity 2.72 (C007). It is more about pricing than fixing the product. It overlaps with the "can't choose songs" complaints, so a joint review is worthwhile.
- **Crashes (playback.p1) and login failures (access.a1):** fewer complaints (3,543 in C021; 2,427 in C029) but more serious (3.39 in C023; 3.92 in C031). Good smaller fixes to run alongside.

## Good to know
- These are historical app-store reviews that people chose to write, so they don't represent all users.
- We analysed a sample: 240,000 of 660,622 reviews.
- There is no revenue or churn data. This shows what users complain about, not money at risk.
